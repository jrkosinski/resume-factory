# Certifications & Awards

## AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate

**Issuer:** Amazon Web Services (AWS)
**Year:** 2023
**Status:** Active (to be confirmed — AWS SAA certifications expire after 3 years)

**Relevance:**
Demonstrates validated knowledge of AWS cloud architecture — compute, storage, networking, databases, security, and cost optimization. Useful for roles involving cloud infrastructure, distributed systems, backend architecture, or any position where AWS familiarity is expected or preferred.

**Resume Use:**
- Safe to list as: *AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate (2023)*
- Strong signal for backend, DevOps, platform, and cloud-adjacent engineering roles
- Relevant ATS keywords: AWS, cloud architecture, Solutions Architect, cloud infrastructure, Amazon Web Services

## Microsoft Certified Trainer (MCT)

**Issuer:** Microsoft
**Year:** 2002
**Status:** Historical (MCT requires annual renewal — likely lapsed; list as held, not current)

**Relevance:**
The MCT credential indicated authorized status to deliver official Microsoft technical training. In 2002 this would have covered .NET, Windows, or related Microsoft technologies — consistent with the Morgan Stanley and HotelTravel era of the career.

**Resume Use:**
- Safe to list as: *Microsoft Certified Trainer (2002)*
- Most useful for historical context or roles where early .NET/Microsoft depth is relevant
- Do not imply the credential is currently active unless renewed
- Relevant ATS keywords: Microsoft Certified, MCT, .NET, Microsoft technologies

---

## Resume Placement Guidance

For most modern roles, list certifications in a compact line at the bottom of the resume or within the TOOLS & TECHNOLOGIES section:

> AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate (2023) | Microsoft Certified Trainer (2002)

For cloud-heavy or AWS-specific roles, consider surfacing the AWS cert more prominently — e.g., as a callout near the top or in the CORE CAPABILITIES section.

# Awards

## EthGlobal Hackathon

**Issuer:** EthGlobal
**Year:** 2023
**Status:** Confirmed

**Relevance:**
EthGlobal runs some of the most prestigious Ethereum and Web3 hackathons in the world, drawing thousands of participants globally. Winning (or placing) at an EthGlobal event is a strong external signal of Solidity/Web3 engineering ability and the capacity to deliver under competitive, time-constrained conditions.

**Resume Use:**
- Safe to list as: *EthGlobal Hackathon Award (2023)*
- Strong signal for Ethereum/Solidity/Web3 roles
- Pairs well with Axiomsys, Garbles Labs, and other blockchain positions
- Relevant ATS keywords: EthGlobal, Ethereum, hackathon, Web3, Solidity, smart contracts

**Project:** Zeppelin — part of the Hamza universe (see CTO-Hamza position)

**Missing / Worth Clarifying:**
- Specific hackathon name (e.g., ETHGlobal Istanbul, ETHGlobal New York, ETHOnline, etc.)
- Prize category or sponsor (many EthGlobal prizes are sponsor-specific: e.g., "Best use of Chainlink", "Best DeFi project")
- Brief description of what Zeppelin was / what it did
- Tech stack used for Zeppelin
- Whether the project was solo or team entry
- Placement or prize tier (winner, finalist, sponsor prize, etc.)

---

## Resume Placement Guidance (Awards)

For Web3 / blockchain roles, list prominently — e.g., alongside certifications or in a dedicated AWARDS section:

> EthGlobal Hackathon Award (2023) | AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate (2023) | Microsoft Certified Trainer (2002)

For non-Web3 roles, the EthGlobal award still signals engineering ability under pressure and can be included without heavy explanation.
