Company: Fidelity Investments, Fidelity Digital Asset Management (FDAM), tokenization team
Role: Vice President, DeFi Product Engineer
Location: Boston, MA, in office (Fidelity is moving to a phased full-time onsite model). No immigration sponsorship.
Compensation: $140,000 to $285,000 base, plus variable pay.

RECRUITER OUTREACH (Heather Williams, Talent Sourcing Strategist, Fidelity Investments):
"We're looking for someone who can bridge product and engineering within the digital assets space, helping build and scale innovative DeFi capabilities while partnering closely with technology and business stakeholders." She asked for a resume and availability for a 15 to 20 minute initial conversation.

ROLE SUMMARY (from the posting on web3.career, paraphrased by the fetcher; full wording at https://web3.career/vice-president-defi-product-engineer-fidelityinvestments/152323):
A senior, hands-on blockchain engineering role at FDAM building smart contracts that support Research, Trading, Risk and Operations. The hire is a senior technical authority who drives infrastructure robustness and leads the tokenization team's technical projects, such as vendor integration, models or tools, automation and analytics.

RESPONSIBILITIES:
1. Architect and oversee blockchain systems and smart contracts across EVM, Solana and Cosmos SDK.
2. Lead design and implementation of crypto infrastructure, including secure deployment pipelines, testing frameworks and audit processes.
3. Run deep technical reviews and audits of smart contracts for security, scalability and DeFi standards compliance.
4. Evaluate and adopt emerging technologies, protocols and tooling.
5. Act as technical liaison across engineering, trading and risk teams.
6. Drive tokenization frameworks, on-chain asset lifecycle management and cross-chain interoperability.
7. Lead evaluation and integration of DeFi protocols, staking, liquidity solutions and institutional-grade custody platforms.
8. Set best practices for blockchain security, smart contract governance, protocol monitoring, threat detection and incident response.

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS:
1. 4+ years of hands-on blockchain engineering, smart contract development or crypto infrastructure work, with a record of technical delivery.
2. Deep understanding of EVM/SVM architecture, token standards (ERC-20, ERC-721, SPL) and DeFi primitives.
3. Proficiency in Python, Solidity and JavaScript/TypeScript.
4. Experience with smart contract tooling such as Hardhat and Foundry.
5. Proven ability to lead complex technical projects and mentor senior engineers.
6. Hands-on Solana development: Anchor, SPL token integrations, PDAs and CPIs.
7. Strong knowledge of DeFi ecosystems: AMMs, lending, liquid staking, yield, oracles and tokenization platforms.
8. Expertise in smart contract security reviews, threat modeling, protocol risk assessment and secure development for institutional digital asset products.
